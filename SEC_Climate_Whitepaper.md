# SEC Climate Disclosure & Compliance Whitepaper

## Introduction
With the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) finalizing rules on climate-related disclosures, publicly traded companies must quantify and report Scope 1, Scope 2, and eventually Scope 3 greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. 

## CarbonVault's Compliance Framework
Titan CarbonVault is engineered specifically for regulatory compliance. Voluntary Carbon Markets (VCMs) are fraught with low-quality, 'phantom' credits. CarbonVault solves this institutional risk by acting as a stringent filtration nexus.

### 1. Zero Double-Counting
Every credit traded through CarbonVault undergoes a cryptographic audit against primary registries (Verra, Gold Standard) to ensure it has not been retired elsewhere. 

### 2. SEC-Ready Reporting
Upon credit retirement, buyers receive compliance-ready documentation containing:
- Vintage Year
- Geographic Origin & Methodology (e.g., Afforestation, Direct Air Capture)
- Registry Verification ID
- Financial clearing timestamps

### 3. Forward-Purchasing Hedging
Offtakers can lock in current market prices for future vintages (e.g., 2026-2030), hedging against the inevitable price surge as SEC mandates come strictly online.
